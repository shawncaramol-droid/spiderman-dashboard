Campaign Overview: The Spiderman brand campaign ran for 32 days (Jul 20 – Aug 20, 2026) across Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat with a total ad spend of $81,677.00. The campaign generated 546 completed orders, 6,390 sessions, and reached 4,881 daily active users. Meta was the dominant platform, driving 56.6% of total orders at a CPO of $2.14. The movie release on Jul 30 created a significant inflection point, with post-release orders increasing 221.9% compared to pre-release.
DAU Clarification: DAU = Daily Active Users (unique per day). The "Peak DAU" shown above represents the highest single-day active user count, not a cumulative sum. Total Users column in source data equals DAU since each row is a unique daily count.
